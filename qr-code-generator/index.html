QR Code Generator

Turn any link or text into a scannable QR code in seconds. Perfect for restaurant menus, business cards, Wi-Fi sharing, event posters and product packaging — then download a clean PNG.

How to Use the QR Code Generator

  1. Type or paste the URL or text you want the code to contain.
  2. Pick a size — 512 × 512 is plenty for most documents and screens; choose 1024 for print.
  3. Click “Generate QR Code” and test it with your phone’s camera.
  4. Click “Download PNG” and use the image anywhere: menus, flyers, labels, slides.

Smart Ways People Use QR Codes

Restaurants link codes on tables to digital menus, cafés share Wi-Fi without spelling out passwords, and realtors put codes on yard signs that open the full listing. Small shops print one on the counter for Google reviews — scans jump review counts because nobody has to type a long URL. Teachers add codes to worksheets, and event organizers link straight to RSVP pages. One rule of thumb: keep the destination short and mobile-friendly, place the code where people can reach it with a phone, and always test your printed code before a big print run. A quiet border of white space around the code helps every scanner read it reliably.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do QR codes generated here expire?

No. The code encodes your text directly in the image, so it works forever as long as the link stays valid.

What size should I print?

At least 1 inch (2.5 cm) square for close-up scanning — larger for posters viewed from a distance.

Can I encode a phone number or Wi-Fi details?

Yes — paste any text, a tel: link or a URL. A scanner reads exactly what you entered.

Is there a scan limit or tracking?

No scan limits and no tracking — the code points straight at your content, not through our site.
