There is no confirmed performance difference between the versions. Use the decision helper below to find the right version for the console you already own — or for no console at all. Is GTA 6 Better on PS5 or Xbox?

There is no confirmed difference. Rockstar has not published an official performance comparison, and no verified frame-rate or resolution difference between PS5 and Xbox Series X has been confirmed. Reports claiming one version runs at 30fps while the other does not are unconfirmed — treat them as rumors, not facts, until Rockstar or a verified technical analysis says otherwise.

That means the deciding factor is not the game, it is you: which console you already own, which controller you prefer, and where your friends will eventually play when multiplayer arrives. If you own a PS5, buy the PS5 version. If you own an Xbox, buy the Xbox version. Buying a second console just for GTA 6 gets you the same game at the same price.

GTA 6 on PS5 and PS5 Pro

GTA 6 launched on PlayStation 5 on November 19, 2026, and the PS5 release covers the PS5 Pro too — there is no separate PS5 Pro version, no Pro-only edition, and no extra purchase. You buy the same PS5 edition ($79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate) whether your console is a standard PS5 or a PS5 Pro. Rockstar has not announced any PS5 Pro-specific mode or enhancement, so do not pay extra for a listing that claims to be a "Pro version" — it does not exist.

GTA 6 on Xbox Series X and Series S

GTA 6 launched on Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S on November 19, 2026, the same day as PS5, at the same prices ($79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate) with the same content. Yes, it runs on Xbox Series S — the cheaper, smaller console plays the full game. If you already own a Series S, it is the cheapest way to play GTA 6: you only need the game itself. No confirmed performance figures for Series S have been published, so nobody can honestly tell you its exact frame rate yet.

What GTA 6 Is NOT On

The launch platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PlayStation 4 version, no Xbox One version, no Nintendo Switch version — and no PC version at launch. If you only have a PS4, an Xbox One or a PC, there is currently no legitimate way to play GTA 6. For the PC wait specifically, see our GTA 6 PC release date page. Any disc, download, APK or "browser version" claiming to be GTA 6 for PS4, Xbox One, Switch or PC is fake — those files are typically malware or scams. The only real copies are on PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store, and at retail.

Download Size and Pre-Load

The download is about 212 GB on every platform, so clear space before you start — you need that much free plus room for updates and save data. Digital pre-load opened on November 10, 2026: PS5 at midnight local time, Xbox at 00:00 GMT. Code-in-box physical copies went on sale from November 12 so buyers could pre-load ahead of launch. Full price details are on our GTA 6 price page, and the live launch timer is on the GTA 6 countdown hub.

Multiplayer and Cross-Play

GTA 6 launched as a single-player story only — Lucia and Jason's story in Leonida and Vice City. There is no multiplayer at launch on any platform, and Rockstar has not announced a GTA VI Online date or any cross-play plans. That means PS5 and Xbox players cannot play together yet, because there is no online mode to play together in. If online play is your main reason for buying, wait for Rockstar's Online announcement before choosing a platform for it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying a second console for the "better" version. There is no confirmed better version. Same game, same price, same content — buy for the console you already own.
  • Paying extra for a "PS5 Pro version." It does not exist. The PS5 edition covers PS5 Pro; any listing charging more for a Pro version is a rip-off.
  • Believing unconfirmed 30fps claims. No verified FPS or resolution difference has been published. Rumors are not benchmarks.
  • Buying a "GTA 6 PC" or "PS4" copy. There is no PC, PS4, Xbox One or Switch version. Those listings and downloads are fake.
  • Expecting cross-play on day one. There is no multiplayer at launch at all, so there is nothing to cross-play in yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GTA 6 on PS5?

Yes. GTA 6 launched on PlayStation 5 on November 19, 2026, including the PS5 Pro — the PS5 release covers both consoles, and there is no separate PS5 Pro version to buy. The Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99, the same price as on Xbox.

Is GTA 6 on Xbox?

Yes. GTA 6 launched on Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S on November 19, 2026, the same day as PS5. The Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99. There is no Xbox One version — the game only runs on the current-generation Series X and Series S.

Is GTA 6 better on PS5 or Xbox?

There is no confirmed difference. Rockstar has not published any official performance comparison, and no verified FPS or resolution difference between PS5 and Xbox Series X has been confirmed — reports claiming one version runs at 30fps while the other does not are unconfirmed. It is the same game, at the same price, with the same content on both platforms, so buy the version for the console you already own.

Does GTA 6 run on Xbox Series S?

Yes. GTA 6 runs on Xbox Series S — it launched on Series S on November 19, 2026, alongside Series X and PS5, with the same content and the same $79.99 Standard Edition price. If you already own a Series S, it is the cheapest way to play; no confirmed performance figures for Series S have been published.

Is there a PS5 Pro version?

There is no separate PS5 Pro version to buy. The PS5 release of GTA 6 covers both the standard PS5 and the PS5 Pro — you buy the same PS5 edition either way, for $79.99 Standard or $99.99 Ultimate. Rockstar has not announced any PS5 Pro-specific mode or enhancement.

Can PS5 and Xbox play GTA 6 together?

No — not at launch. GTA 6 launched as a single-player story only, with no multiplayer at all, so there is nothing to play together yet. Rockstar has also not announced cross-play for the multiplayer mode that is expected to arrive later.

Should I buy a PS5 just for GTA 6?

Only if you want the best single-player experience and are happy paying for a whole console for one game. If you already own any supported console — PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X or Xbox Series S — buy the game for that console instead; it is the same game at the same price.
