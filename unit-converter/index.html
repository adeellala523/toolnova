Convert between metric and imperial units without the mental math. Length, weight and temperature — pick a category, enter a value, and swap direction with one click.

How to Use the Unit Converter

  1. Select a category: length, weight or temperature.
  2. Type the value and choose the “from” and “to” units.
  3. Read the result instantly — it updates as you type.
  4. Use “Swap Units” to reverse the conversion without re-entering anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. Example: 20°C = 68°F. The temperature mode also handles Kelvin.

How many feet are in a meter?

About 3.281 feet. One inch is exactly 2.54 cm by international definition.

How do I convert pounds to kilograms?

Divide pounds by 2.20462. One pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg.

Are results exact?

Yes — official definitions are used, with up to 6 decimal places shown.
