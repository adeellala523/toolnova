GTA 6 PC Release Date: Is GTA 6 on PC?

The GTA 6 PC release date does not exist yet — because Rockstar Games has not announced a PC version of Grand Theft Auto VI at all. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and PC players are still waiting for even a confirmation, let alone a date. This page gives you the honest answer: what Rockstar and Take-Two have actually said, the unconfirmed report of a first-quarter 2027 PC window, what the console hardware tells us about the PC you will need, and a free checker that compares your PC against the PS5 the game was built for. All facts checked on November 20, 2026.

    Why There Is No GTA 6 PC Release Date Yet

    Rockstar has launched its last two huge games on console first and PC later, and it has said nothing different this time. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's only on-record line is that "Rockstar Games has announced GTA 6 for console only so far" — a careful sentence that neither confirms nor denies a PC version. The Rockstar Store sells only the PS5 and Xbox versions, there is no Steam, Epic or Rockstar Launcher listing, and Take-Two's own release slate through March 31, 2027 lists GTA VI for PS5 and Xbox only. Until Rockstar itself announces a PC version, every date you see online is a guess or a report — not a fact. For the full launch story, see our GTA 6 Countdown hub.

    The Unconfirmed Q1 2027 PC Report

    The most specific PC claim anywhere comes from GamesBeat: on October 13, 2026, it reported, citing four anonymous industry sources, that a PC version is planned for the first quarter of 2027. Rockstar did not comment. That makes it an unconfirmed report, not a date — and it is worth remembering that Rockstar's PC versions have historically arrived well over a year after console launch, not a single quarter later. Treat Q1 2027 as a rumor to watch, not a date to plan a PC build around.

    Can You Play GTA 6 on PC Through Cloud Gaming?

    Not as of November 19, 2026. There is no GeForce NOW announcement, no PlayStation Plus cloud-streaming version for PC, and no Xbox Cloud Gaming version of GTA 6. The game only runs natively on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so there is currently no legal way to play it on a PC — streamed or otherwise. If a cloud option is announced, it will come from Rockstar, NVIDIA or Sony, not from a random download site.

    Warning: fake "GTA 6 APK / PC download" sites are scams. Any site offering a GTA 6 APK, a PC installer, a "free download" or a browser version is fake — there is no official PC version to download, and those files are typically malware or scams built to steal accounts and passwords. The only real copies are on PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store, and at retail. If a site asks you to download an installer, complete a survey, or enter your Rockstar or Steam login to "unlock" GTA 6 on PC, close it.
