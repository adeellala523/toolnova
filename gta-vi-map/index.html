GTA 6 Map Size: What Rockstar Has (and Hasn't) Said

Here is the honest answer on GTA 6 map size: Rockstar has published no official size figure. There is no confirmed number in square kilometres or miles, and no official comparison to GTA V's map — so claims like "twice the size of GTA V" are speculation, not fact, however often they are repeated.

What we do know is the shape of it. Leonida is a full state, not a single city map: six regions, five counties, a major city, an island chain, wetlands, farmland and a national park. The story itself proves the scale — it opens in the Leonida Keys and, by GameSpot's account, roughly the first 20% of it plays there before the action moves to Vice City. A map that needs a fifth of its story just to leave the starting islands is a big map by any measure — but until Rockstar publishes a number, the size is "very large", not a figure.

Vice City Neighbourhoods on the GTA 6 Map

Vice City is the one region most players will know best, and Rockstar has named eight of its neighbourhoods and landmarks. If you are planning where to go first, these are the places to look for on the GTA 6 Vice City map:

  • Ocean Beach — the famous beachfront strip, Miami Beach in all but name.
  • Little Cuba — the Cuban neighbourhood, Little Havana's counterpart.
  • Stockyard — the industrial, working-class side of the city.
  • La Pearl — a coastal neighbourhood along the bay.
  • Downtown — the skyscraper core of Vice City.
  • Washington Beach — another beachfront district, south of Ocean Beach.
  • Port Vice City — the docks and container port.
  • VCIA airport — Vice City International Airport, the way in and out by air.

The 5 Counties of Leonida

Rockstar's official printable map, published on launch day — November 19, 2026 — divides Leonida into five counties: Lummox, Leonard, Kelly, Vice-Dale and Mariana. Rockstar has not published a county-by-county breakdown showing which region or city sits in which county, so the exact borders between them are still unconfirmed. The names themselves are the clues players have for now — Vice-Dale, for instance, is the natural guess for Vice City's county, but that is a guess from the name, not an official fact.

How to Use This GTA 6 Map

  • Start in the Keys. The story begins around Key Lento, so learn the islands, the boats and Brian Heder's boat yard first — you will spend roughly the first fifth of the story there.
  • Then head for Vice City. It is the mission hub and the biggest concentration of activities, shops and neighbourhoods on the map.
  • Take an airboat through the Grassrivers. The wetlands are the fastest way to cross the south-west — and the most dangerous, with alligators in the water.
  • Go north when you want a break from the city. Mount Kalaga National Park is built for hunting, fishing and off-road driving, far from Vice City's neon.
  • Check the story, not just the map. Our GTA 6 characters guide covers Lucia, Jason and the people you will meet in each region, and the GTA 6 beginner tips page covers what to do in your first hours. The GTA 6 countdown hub has the launch facts, prices and PC news.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Treating Vice City as the whole map. It is one of six regions — most of Leonida is outside the city limits.
  • Trusting a map-size number. Rockstar has published no size figure. Any exact number or "2x GTA V" claim is made up until Rockstar says otherwise.
  • Using a fan-made "leaked" map as fact. Only Rockstar's official printable map (November 19, 2026) and its region descriptions are confirmed. Everything else is guesswork, however detailed it looks.
  • Assuming the old May 26, 2026 date matters. That was the first delay's date — the game actually launched on November 19, 2026, and the map above is the launch-day Leonida.

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has published no official map-size figure — no square kilometres, no miles, and no comparison such as 'twice GTA V', so any number you see online is speculation. What is confirmed is that Leonida is a full US state, much larger than Vice City alone: it holds six regions, five counties, a major city, wetlands, islands, farmland and a national park. Rockstar's own printable map, published on November 19, 2026, shows the whole state at a glance.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. The game launched on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its story follows Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval across the state — from Vice City on the southeast coast to the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands and the northern national park.

What are the 6 regions of Leonida?

Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Vice City is the Miami-style city on the southeast coast; the Keys are the southern island chain; Grassrivers is the Everglades-style wetlands; Port Gellhorn is a decaying west-coast resort town; Ambrosia is the sugar-refinery town beside Lake Leonida; and Mount Kalaga National Park covers the northern border.

Is Vice City the whole map?

No — Vice City is only one of six regions. It is the biggest city and the centre of the action, with neighbourhoods including Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Stockyard, La Pearl, Downtown, Washington Beach, Port Vice City and the VCIA airport, but most of Leonida lies outside it: the Keys, the Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park are all separate, explorable regions.

Can you visit the Leonida Keys?

Yes. The Leonida Keys are a playable region at the southern tip of the map — a chain of islands inspired by the Florida Keys. The story itself starts there: Lucia and Jason's story opens in the Keys around Key Lento, at Brian Heder's boat yard, and GameSpot reports that roughly the first 20% of the story plays out in the Keys before the action moves to Vice City.

Is there an official GTA 6 map?

Yes. Rockstar published an official printable map of Leonida on November 19, 2026 — launch day — at rockstargames.com/VI. It divides the state into five counties: Lummox, Leonard, Kelly, Vice-Dale and Mariana. It is a stylised overview rather than a detailed street map, and this page's interactive map is an original schematic based on Rockstar's region descriptions, not a copy of Rockstar's art.

What county is Vice City in?

Rockstar's official map divides Leonida into five counties — Lummox, Leonard, Kelly, Vice-Dale and Mariana — but Rockstar has not published a county-by-county breakdown saying which county each city or region belongs to, so there is no confirmed answer for Vice City yet. The most likely candidate by name is Vice-Dale, but that is an educated guess from the name, not an official fact.
