How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick the date of birth (or any start date) in the first field.
  2. Leave the second field as today, or set another date to check age at that moment.
  3. Click “Calculate Age” to see the full breakdown.
  4. Use the totals for forms that ask for age in days, weeks or completed years.

Why Exact Age Matters

Plenty of official decisions hinge on precise age, not just the year you were born. School admissions use cut-off dates (“must be 5 by September 1”), sports leagues group kids by exact age, and employers and pension plans calculate eligibility in completed years and months. Drivers’ licenses, voting, insurance premiums and medical dosing can all depend on the exact figure. Counting by hand across leap years is error-prone — a person born in 2000 has lived through seven leap days already. This calculator handles the calendar math for you, including month lengths and leap years, and works for any pair of dates: try two historical dates to measure the span between events.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my exact age calculated?

We subtract your birth date from the target date and split the difference into full years, full months, then remaining days — the same method used on official forms.

Can I calculate age at a future or past date?

Yes. Change the “age at date” field to any date — useful for school cut-offs, retirement planning or visa checks.

What is “total days alive”?

The number of days from your birth date to the selected date, leap days included. We also show total weeks.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser only; nothing is sent to a server.
