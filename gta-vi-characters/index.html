GTA 6 Characters: Lucia, Jason & the Full Cast

The GTA 6 characters are led by Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a criminal duo at the heart of Grand Theft Auto VI, which launched on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has published eight official character profiles — from paranoid Coast Guard radio listener Cal Hampton to Vice City music mogul Boobie Ike — and this page covers every one of them, plus the quiz below that tells you which GTA 6 character you are.

Five quick questions, six possible results — Lucia, Jason, Cal, Boobie, Raul or Brian. Based on Rockstar's eight official character profiles, published May 6, 2025.

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval: The Dual Protagonists

Grand Theft Auto VI is built around two leads you play as: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. The story starts when an easy score goes wrong, and the pair get pulled into a criminal conspiracy that stretches across Leonida — Rockstar's fictional US state — and its neon-soaked city of Vice City, a modern version of Miami. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so both characters are playable right now.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia's father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk, and she has fought for her family ever since — a fight that landed her in Leonida Penitentiary. Now newly out, she is back on the outside with Liberty City roots behind her and nothing left to lose. She is the first woman to headline a mainline GTA story, and she is linked to Jason — the two are a team, not a hero and a sidekick.

Jason Duval

Jason is an Army veteran who works for drug runners in the Florida Keys. He wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder — and his story is tied to Lucia, to his paranoid friend Cal Hampton, and to his landlord Brian Heder. Jason is the steady one of the pair: practical, loyal, and always the person holding things together when a plan falls apart.

Cal Hampton

Cal is Jason's paranoid stay-at-home friend — the kind of person who listens to Coast Guard radio for fun and assumes someone is always watching. He is an associate of Brian Heder, and he is the comic relief of the cast with a genuinely anxious streak underneath.

Brian Heder

Brian is a veteran drug runner from the old school who now runs a boat yard in the Keys. He is Jason's landlord — Jason lives on his property — and he is on his third wife, Lori. Brian knows every back channel in the Keys, and Jason's work for the runners runs through people like him.

Raul Bautista

Raul is a seasoned bank robber whose motto is "Experience counts." He recruits risk-takers for big jobs, and he is the character most likely to be behind the score that goes wrong for Lucia and Jason. If anyone in Leonida knows how a heist should run, it is Raul.

Boobie Ike

Boobie is a Vice City legend who turned street success into a business empire: real estate, a strip club and the recording studio and label Only Raw Records. He is the money and the connections side of Vice City's music scene — and the man Dre'Quan Priest and Real Dimez answer to.

Dre'Quan Priest

Dre'Quan is a music mogul in the making and Boobie Ike's partner at Only Raw Records. He is the ambitious one — building the label, signing the talent, and trying to turn Vice City's sound into something bigger.

Real Dimez

Real Dimez are the rap duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to Only Raw Records. They bring the music of Vice City to life in the game, and their rise runs through Boobie's label.

Who Voices the GTA 6 Characters?

Rockstar has not announced the voice actors for Lucia, Jason or any other GTA 6 character. Any names you see on social media or fan sites are guesses, not official casting — Rockstar has kept the cast secret since the first trailer. If and when Rockstar announces the voices, this page will be updated.

Do Any GTA 5 Characters Return in GTA 6?

No returning characters have been announced. Rockstar has not confirmed any GTA 5 character appearing in GTA 6, and all eight official character profiles are new faces. That does not rule out a surprise cameo, but as of launch there is no confirmed link between the GTA 5 cast and Leonida.

New Faces From the Extended Look

Rockstar's Extended Look, released August 27, 2026, added more named faces to the world of Leonida beyond the original eight profiles — more of the people Lucia and Jason cross paths with as the conspiracy spreads. The eight May 2025 profiles remain the core cast, but the Extended Look showed the world around them is much bigger. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Believing fan-cast voice actors. Rockstar has announced no voice cast. Lists of "confirmed" actors for Lucia and Jason are speculation.
  • Expecting GTA 5 characters to show up. No returning characters have been announced — the GTA 6 cast is new.
  • Calling Lucia a side character. She is a full co-lead: GTA 6 is a dual-protagonist story, and Lucia is the first woman to headline a mainline GTA game.
  • Trusting the old May 26, 2026 date. That was the first delay's date, announced May 2, 2025 — Rockstar moved the game again to November 19, 2026, the date it actually launched on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. They are the game's dual protagonists — a criminal duo from Leonida whose story begins when an easy score goes wrong and pulls them into a conspiracy across the state. You play as both of them.

Is Lucia the first woman to lead a mainline GTA story?

Yes. Lucia Caminos is the first female lead protagonist of a mainline Grand Theft Auto story. Earlier mainline GTA games had male leads, but Lucia is the first woman to headline a mainline entry.

Can you play as both Lucia and Jason?

Yes. GTA 6 has dual protagonists — you play as both Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, switching between them as the story moves across Leonida.

Who is the villain in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not announced a single villain for GTA 6. The story is built around Lucia and Jason being pulled into a conspiracy across Leonida after a score goes wrong — no main antagonist has been named.

Are any GTA 5 characters in GTA 6?

No returning characters have been announced. Rockstar has not confirmed any GTA 5 character appearing in GTA 6, and the eight official character profiles are all new faces.

Who voices Lucia and Jason?

Rockstar has not announced the voice actors for Lucia, Jason or any other GTA 6 character. Any names you see online are fan guesses, not official casting.

How many characters does Rockstar profile?

Eight. Rockstar published eight official character profiles on May 6, 2025, alongside Trailer 2: Lucia Caminos, Jason Duval, Cal Hampton, Brian Heder, Raul Bautista, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest and Real Dimez.
