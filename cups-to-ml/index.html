How Cups to Ml Conversion Works

Cups and milliliters both measure volume, but they come from two different systems: the cup is a US customary unit, the milliliter is metric. The two are linked by one exact definition: 1 US cup is exactly 236.5882365 milliliters. That gives the conversion factor every calculator uses:

  1. Cups to ml: multiply by 236.588. Milliliters = cups × 236.588. So 2 cups × 236.588 = 473.18 ml.
  2. Ml to cups: divide by 236.588. So 250 ml ÷ 236.588 = 1.06 cups.
  3. Cups to tablespoons: multiply by 16. One cup holds 16 tablespoons, and each tablespoon is 3 teaspoons — so 1 cup = 48 teaspoons.

One warning before you trust any number: not every "cup" is the same size. The US cup (236.59 ml) is what American recipes mean. The metric cup (250 ml) is used in Australia and some other countries. And the US legal cup (240 ml) appears on American nutrition labels. The difference between a US cup and a metric cup is about 13 ml per cup — small for one cup of milk, but over 50 ml across a 4-cup cake batter, enough to change the result.

A Worked Example

A baker finds an American pancake recipe calling for 2 cups of milk, but her jug is marked in milliliters. Multiply: 2 × 236.588 = 473.18 ml — type 2 into the converter above and it confirms exactly that, with 32 tablespoons alongside.

The reverse check works the same way. A European recipe calls for 250 ml of cream and her only measure is a US cup: divide, 250 ÷ 236.588 = 1.06 cups, so she pours just over one cup. And a full litre is 1000 ÷ 236.588 = 4.23 cups.

Common measures, for a quick sense of the scale: ¼ cup = 59.15 ml, ⅓ cup = 78.86 ml, ½ cup = 118.29 ml, ⅔ cup = 157.73 ml, ¾ cup = 177.44 ml, 1 cup = 236.59 ml, 2 cups = 473.18 ml, 3 cups = 709.76 ml, 4 cups = 946.35 ml.

Tips for Converting Cups to Ml

  • Check which cup the recipe means first. A US recipe means 236.59 ml; an Australian recipe usually means 250 ml. If the recipe lists grams as well, trust the grams — weight never depends on which cup the author used.
  • Use the full 236.588 for baking, round for cooking. Baking is chemistry: a 13 ml error per cup matters. For a soup or a marinade, "about 475 ml" for 2 cups is fine.
  • Remember 1 cup = 16 tablespoons. No cup measure to hand? 16 level tablespoons make a cup, and 3 teaspoons make a tablespoon. The converter shows the tablespoon count for exactly this reason.
  • Don't convert dry and liquid cups the same way. A cup of flour and a cup of water have the same volume (236.59 ml) but very different weights — about 120 g of flour against 236 g of water. Volume converts; weight does not follow from it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using 250 ml for a US recipe. The metric cup is 13 ml bigger, so a 4-cup recipe converted at 250 ml per cup adds an extra 54 ml — enough to make a cake batter too wet.
  • Mixing up the US legal cup (240 ml). Nutrition labels use a 240 ml cup, which is neither the recipe cup (236.59 ml) nor the metric cup (250 ml). Use 236.588 for recipes.
  • Converting weight from volume. "2 cups = 500 g" is only true for water (and near enough for milk). For flour, sugar or butter, look up that ingredient's own cup-to-gram weight — the converter measures volume, not mass.
  • Rounding in the middle. Converting 2 cups → 473 ml → back to cups using the rounded 473 gives 2.00 cups, which is fine — but rounding ⅓ cup to 79 ml and then scaling it up to a tripled recipe compounds the error. Round only at the very end.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many ml is 1 cup?

1 US cup is 236.59 ml. That is the cup used in American recipes. A metric cup, used in Australia and some other countries, is 250 ml — so always check which cup your recipe means before converting.

How do I convert cups to ml?

Multiply the cups by 236.59. For example, 2 cups × 236.59 = 473.18 ml. The converter above does this instantly for any amount you type in, and it also shows the result in tablespoons and teaspoons.

How many cups is 250 ml?

250 ml is 1.06 US cups — just over one cup. To convert milliliters back to cups, divide by 236.59: 250 ÷ 236.59 = 1.06. The reverse section of the converter above does this for any milliliter value.

How many ml is 3/4 cup?

3/4 cup is 177.44 ml. Multiply 0.75 × 236.59 = 177.44. Other common fractions: 1/4 cup = 59.15 ml, 1/3 cup = 78.86 ml, 1/2 cup = 118.29 ml, 2/3 cup = 157.73 ml.

Is a US cup the same as a metric cup?

No. A US cup is 236.59 ml, while a metric cup is exactly 250 ml — a difference of about 13 ml per cup, which adds up in baking. A US legal cup, used on American nutrition labels, is 240 ml. Recipes from the US almost always mean the 236.59 ml cup; recipes from Australia usually mean the 250 ml metric cup.

How many tablespoons are in a cup?

There are 16 tablespoons in 1 US cup, and 3 teaspoons in 1 tablespoon — so 1 cup is 48 teaspoons. One tablespoon is 14.79 ml and one teaspoon is 4.93 ml.
