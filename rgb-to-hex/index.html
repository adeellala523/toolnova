How RGB to HEX Conversion Works

An RGB color is three numbers, each from 0 to 255 — one for red, one for green, one for blue. A hex code is the same three numbers written in hexadecimal (base 16) instead of decimal (base 10), joined together after a #. Converting is a matter of translating each channel from base 10 to base 16.

  1. Convert each channel to hex. Divide the number by 16: the quotient is the first hex digit, the remainder is the second. 124 ÷ 16 is 7 remainder 12 — and 12 in hex is C — so 124 becomes 7C.
  2. Pad single digits with a leading zero. A channel below 16 converts to one hex digit, but a hex code needs two per channel: 14 becomes 0E, 5 becomes 05, 0 becomes 00.
  3. Join the three pairs after a #. 14, 124, 134 become 0E, 7C and 86, joined as #0E7C86.

A Worked Example

A developer copies a teal from a design file: rgb(14, 124, 134). The style guide wants hex, so each channel converts: 14 is 0E (14 ÷ 16 is 0 remainder 14, and 14 in hex is E), 124 is 7C (7 remainder 12, and 12 is C), 134 is 86 (8 remainder 6). Joined, that is #0E7C86 — type the three numbers above and the converter confirms exactly that, with hsl(185, 81%, 29%) alongside.

The leading-zero rule is what trips people up: the red channel here is 14, which is a single hex digit (E) on its own. Without the padding zero it would come out as #E7C86 — five digits, which no color picker accepts. A second check with a warm accent, rgb(255, 87, 51), gives #FF5733 (255 is FF, 87 is 57, 51 is 33); and rgb(255, 85, 51) gives #FF5533, the expanded form of the shorthand #F53.

Tips for Using RGB and HEX Together

  • Keep the hex code as the source of truth. Hex is shorter to store in a style guide, and RGB is derived from it in one step — never maintain two separate lists that can drift apart. Or open the full Color Converter to compare HEX, RGB and HSL side by side.
  • Use RGBA for overlays, shadows and hovers. rgba(14, 124, 134, 0.5) tints without hiding what sits underneath; a flat hex color cannot do that. The 8-digit hex form #0E7C8680 works in modern browsers, but rgba() is safer.
  • Check the swatch, not just the numbers. A typo in one RGB value can shift a color subtly (124 vs 125 is invisible in numbers, visible on screen). The live swatch above catches it at a glance.
  • Remember the padding rule. Any channel below 16 needs a leading zero — 14 is 0E, not E. Forgetting it produces a 5-digit code that breaks color pickers.
  • Copy the HEX, don't retype it. One transposed digit turns a brand teal into a stranger's blue. The copy buttons above paste the exact value.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the leading zero. rgb(14, 124, 134) is #0E7C86, not #E7C86. A hex code must be exactly six digits (plus the #).
  • Mixing up the channel order. rgb(255, 87, 51) is red 255, green 87, blue 51 — #FF5733, not #3357FF. Swapping red and blue turns an orange into a blue.
  • Treating hex digits as decimal. 7C is not 7 × 10 + 12 = 82 — it is 7 × 16 + 12 = 124. Reading hex pairs as ordinary numbers is the most common conversion error.
  • Pasting a hex code into an RGB field. #FF5733 and rgb(255, 87, 51) describe the same color but are different notations — a field expecting three numbers will reject the hex form. Convert first, then paste.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert RGB to HEX?

Convert each RGB number (0–255) to a two-digit hexadecimal number, then join the three pairs with a # in front. For example, rgb(255, 87, 51) becomes #FF5733, because 255 is FF, 87 is 57 and 51 is 33 in hex. The converter above does this instantly for any RGB values you type in.

What is the HEX code for rgb(14, 124, 134)?

#0E7C86. The three channels convert as 14 → 0E, 124 → 7C and 134 → 86, giving the deep teal used across this site. Type 14, 124, 134 into the converter above to see it with its RGBA and HSL values and a live swatch.

What happens to RGB numbers below 16, like 14?

They get a leading zero so every channel is exactly two hex digits. 14 becomes 0E, 5 becomes 05 and 0 becomes 00 — so rgb(5, 10, 15) is #050A0F, not #5AF. Without the padding, a code would be shorter than six digits and a color picker would read it wrong.

How do I convert HEX back to RGB?

Split the hex code into three pairs of digits and convert each pair from hexadecimal (base 16) to decimal (base 10). For example, #FF5733 splits into FF, 57 and 33, which are 255, 87 and 51 in decimal, so the RGB value is rgb(255, 87, 51). The converter above has a HEX-to-RGB section that does this for you.

Why would I need HEX when I already have the RGB values?

Because HEX is the shorthand almost every design tool and style guide uses. Brand guides list #0E7C86, not rgb(14, 124, 134); color pickers, CSS variables and design files all accept hex directly, and it is shorter to store and share. RGB is the form you need for transparency (rgba), hex is the form you keep in the style guide.

Can I convert RGBA (with transparency) to HEX?

Yes, with 8-digit hex: convert the alpha value the same way as a color channel, 0 to 1 becoming 00 to FF. rgba(14, 124, 134, 0.5) is #0E7C8680, because 0.5 × 255 rounds to 128, which is 80 in hex. Modern browsers accept 8-digit hex in CSS, but older ones do not — rgba() is still the safer choice for transparency.

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser and nothing you type leaves your device. You can convert brand colors or unreleased palette codes here without anything being sent or saved anywhere.
