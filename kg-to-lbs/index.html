How Kg to Lbs Conversion Works

Kilograms and pounds measure the same thing — mass — in two different systems. The kilogram is the metric unit used by almost every country; the pound is the imperial unit still used in the United States (and, for body weight, in the UK alongside the stone). The two are linked by one exact definition: since 1959, 1 pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. Flip that around and you get the conversion factor every calculator uses:

  1. Kg to lbs: multiply by 2.20462. Pounds = kilograms × 2.20462. So 70 kg × 2.20462 = 154.32 lbs.
  2. Lbs to kg: divide by 2.20462 (or multiply by 0.45359237). So 150 lbs ÷ 2.20462 = 68.04 kg.
  3. Pounds to stone: divide by 14. One stone is exactly 14 pounds, so 154.32 lbs ÷ 14 = 11.02 stone.

A Worked Example

A traveller steps on a hotel scale in London and reads 70 kg, but her US airline's baggage form and her fitness app both want pounds. Multiply: 70 × 2.20462 = 154.32 lbs — type 70 into the converter above and it confirms exactly that, with 11.02 stone alongside for a UK doctor's form.

The reverse check works the same way. A gym program written in the US says to deadlift 150 lbs; divide by the same factor, 150 ÷ 2.20462 = 68.04 kg, so she loads 68 kg on the bar. And the numbers tie into health too: at 70 kg and 1.75 m tall, her BMI is 70 ÷ 1.75² = 22.9, inside the healthy 18.5–24.9 range.

Common weights, for a quick sense of the scale: 50 kg = 110.23 lbs, 60 kg = 132.28 lbs, 70 kg = 154.32 lbs, 80 kg = 176.37 lbs, 90 kg = 198.42 lbs, 100 kg = 220.46 lbs.

Tips for Converting Weights

  • Use the full 2.20462 for anything that matters. Airline luggage limits, gym records and medical forms all deserve the precise factor — the 2.2 shortcut is nearly half a pound off at 100 kg.
  • Round to one decimal for body weight, two for luggage. Nobody's weight is 154.3234 lbs; "154.3 lbs" is honest precision. For a 23 kg bag limit, two decimals keep you safely under the line.
  • Remember the stone for UK body weight. A British doctor, gym or clothes size chart may ask for stone — divide pounds by 14. 70 kg is 11 stone, not 154 of anything.
  • Check which unit a form wants before you type. Entering 70 into a pounds field when it expects kilograms (or the reverse) is a 2.2× error — the most common weight-form mistake there is.
  • Pair it with BMI. Weight alone says little about health; the same 70 kg is a different story at 1.60 m than at 1.90 m. Our BMI calculator takes the same number and gives the category.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using 2.2 when precision matters. It is fine for a rough guess, but 100 kg × 2.2 gives 220 lbs against the true 220.46 lbs — enough to tip a bag over an airline's limit.
  • Dividing when you should multiply. Kg to lbs is multiply by 2.20462; lbs to kg is divide. Swapping them turns 70 kg into 31.75 lbs — lighter than a toddler.
  • Confusing stone with pounds. 11 stone is 154 lbs, not 11 lbs. Always check whether a UK figure is in stone (st) or pounds (lb) before converting.
  • Trusting a rounded intermediate. Converting 70 kg → 154 lbs → back to kg using the rounded 154 gives 69.85 kg, not 70. Round only at the very end, never in the middle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many lbs is 1 kg?

1 kilogram is 2.20462 pounds. For everyday use, 2.2 is close enough — 70 kg is about 154 lbs — but for luggage limits and precise records, use the full 2.20462: 70 kg is exactly 154.32 lbs.

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Multiply the kilograms by 2.20462. For example, 70 kg × 2.20462 = 154.32 lbs. The converter above does this instantly for any weight you type in, and it also shows the result in stones.

How many kg is 150 lbs?

150 lbs is 68.04 kg. To convert pounds back to kilograms, divide by 2.20462: 150 ÷ 2.20462 = 68.04. The reverse section of the converter above does this for any pound value.

What is 70 kg in stone and pounds?

70 kg is 154.32 lbs, which is 11.02 stone — just over 11 stone. One stone is exactly 14 pounds, so divide the pounds by 14 to get stones: 154.32 ÷ 14 = 11.02 st.

Is multiplying by 2.2 accurate enough?

For everyday estimates, yes — the error is about 0.2%. But it adds up: 100 kg × 2.2 gives 220 lbs, while the true value is 220.46 lbs, nearly half a pound off. For airline luggage, gym records and medical forms, use 2.20462.

Why do the US and UK use different weight units?

The US still uses the imperial pound for body weight, food and luggage, while most of the world — including the UK for official and medical use — uses kilograms. The UK adds a third unit for body weight: the stone (14 lbs). That is why the same person can be 70 kg, 154 lbs, or 11 stone depending on who is asking.

Is my weight data uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser and nothing you type leaves your device. You can convert your own body weight here without anything being sent or saved anywhere.
