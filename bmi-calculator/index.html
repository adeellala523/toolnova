Understanding the BMI Scale

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9Overweight
30.0 and aboveObese

BMI is a quick screening tool used by doctors, insurers and fitness programs worldwide because it only needs height and weight. It does have limits: it cannot tell muscle from fat, so bodybuilders may score “overweight” while being very lean, and it does not show where fat is carried. Treat your result as a starting point for a conversation about health — alongside waist size, activity level, blood pressure and how you actually feel — rather than a verdict.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is the healthy range. Below that is underweight; 25–29.9 is overweight; 30+ is obese.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². The imperial version is 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)².

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

It can overestimate body fat in muscular people. It is a screening guide, not a diagnosis — see a clinician for the full picture.

Can children use this calculator?

Kids need age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles. This calculator applies the adult categories.
