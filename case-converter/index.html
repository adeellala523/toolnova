The Cases, Explained

UPPERCASE suits labels, warnings and acronyms — but a whole paragraph in capitals reads like shouting and is harder to scan. lowercase is the fastest fix for text typed with Caps Lock stuck on. Title Case capitalizes every word and is the classic style for headlines, book titles and video names. Sentence case capitalizes only the first word of each sentence (and is what most modern websites use for headings). Capitalize Each Word is similar to Title Case but also capitalizes small words like “a” and “the”. aLtErNaTiNg case is just for fun — memes and playful posts. Choosing the right case makes writing look deliberate and professional instead of accidental.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word, like a headline: “The Quick Brown Fox.”

Sentence case vs. Title Case — what differs?

Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence; Title Case capitalizes every word.

Can it fix accidental CAPS LOCK text?

Yes — paste it, click “Sentence case” or “lowercase,” and copy the fixed text back.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser.
