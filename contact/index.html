  • Bug reports: tell us which tool you used, what you entered, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your device and browser (for example “iPhone, Safari”) help a lot.

What We Can and Can’t Do

Because ToolNova tools run entirely in your browser, we never receive or store your data — which also means we cannot recover text, images or results for you. If a page was closed, the work is gone; please copy important results before leaving a tool page. We also cannot provide medical, financial or legal advice: the calculators give general estimates, and the disclaimer page explains this in full.
