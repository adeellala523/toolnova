Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026. This policy explains what information ToolNova (“we”, “the site”) does and does not collect when you visit https://adeellala523.github.io/toolnova/ (the “Site”).

1. Data Processed by the Tools

All tools on ToolNova run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Text you type or paste, images you open, and values you enter are processed on your own device only. They are not transmitted to, stored on, or accessible from any server operated by us. We have no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and no database of visitor content. If you close a tool page, the data you entered is discarded by your browser.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and operational purposes. Those logs are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement, available at github.com.

3. Cookies

ToolNova itself does not set cookies. However, third-party services described below (advertising and, if enabled, analytics) may set cookies or use similar technologies (web beacons, device identifiers) when you visit the Site.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

We use or plan to use Google AdSense to display advertising, which keeps the tools free. In connection with serving ads:

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page (optout.networkadvertising.org).
  • For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, ads may be served in non-personalized mode where required by law or by the settings of Google’s consent tools.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics to understand which tools are popular so we can improve them. Aggregate statistics (such as page views by country) do not identify individual visitors, and tool inputs are never part of analytics data.

6. Links to Other Websites

The Site may link to external websites (for example, resources in articles). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

ToolNova is a general-audience utility site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). As noted above, we do not collect personal information from any visitor.

8. Your Choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the tools will continue to work. You can opt out of personalized advertising using the links in section 4.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at maliklaladeel@gmail.com or visit the contact page.
