A 30-Second History of Lorem Ipsum

The text traces back to Cicero’s “de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum” (45 BC), scrambled by a typesetter in the 1500s to make a type specimen book. It survived into the 20th century on Letraset transfer sheets, then became the default dummy text in desktop publishing software. Designers keep using it for one good reason: it mimics the rhythm and letter distribution of ordinary English without saying anything, so clients and users judge the design itself instead of reading the copy. Use it anywhere real content is not ready yet — landing page drafts, app onboarding screens, brochure layouts — and swap in final text before launch (a step famously forgotten by more than a few websites).
