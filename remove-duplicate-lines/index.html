How to Use the Duplicate Line Remover

  1. Paste your text into the input box — one item per line works best.
  2. Choose options: sort the result, ignore letter case, trim spaces, drop blank lines.
  3. Click “Remove Duplicates” and check the before/after counts.
  4. Copy the clean list back into your spreadsheet, document or tool.

Everyday Jobs This Finishes in Seconds

Duplicates sneak into every kind of list. Merge two mailing lists and hundreds of subscribers appear twice — send to them twice and you look like spam. Combine keyword exports from different SEO tools and the same phrase shows up in five rows. Developers dedupe log files, user IDs and configuration entries; warehouse teams clean SKU and barcode lists before inventory counts; teachers merge class rosters from multiple sources. Doing it by hand in a spreadsheet means sorting, eyeballing and risking a missed row. This tool compares every line exactly (or loosely, with the case and trim options) and shows you precisely how many repeats it removed, so you can trust the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?

It keeps the first occurrence of each unique line and removes every repeat. Options let you sort, ignore case and trim spaces first.

What counts as a duplicate?

Identical lines. With “ignore case” on, “Apple” and “apple” match; with “trim spaces” on, surrounding spaces are ignored too.

Can it handle a large list?

Yes — it runs locally and handles tens of thousands of lines, from mailing lists to log files.

Is my list uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device.
