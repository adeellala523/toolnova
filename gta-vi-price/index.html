Why $79.99? The Price in Context

The GTA 6 price of $79.99 is $10 above the usual $69.99 AAA price that became standard with the PS5 and Xbox Series X generation — but it is also below the rumored $100 base price that circulated for years before the announcement. Rockstar chose a middle path: a new, higher price point for the biggest game of the generation, without going all the way to triple figures for the base edition. The Ultimate Edition at $99.99 is where the $100 figure actually landed — as the premium edition, not the standard one.

Whether $79.99 becomes the new normal for AAA games is still an open question. For now, GTA 6 is the highest-profile game to charge it, and its sales will be watched across the industry as a test of what players will accept.

What Is in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition price is $99.99 (£89.99, AU$159.95) — $20 more than Standard. It includes the full game plus digital extras, including the Vintage Vice City Pack. If you only want the game itself, the Standard Edition at $79.99 has the same story and the same Vice City to explore; the Ultimate Edition is for players who want the extra digital content on top.

Before you buy, check the edition details on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store listing — that is the authoritative list of what each edition includes, and it is the place to confirm the extras before spending the extra $20.

What Is the Vice City Collection?

The Vice City Collection is a physical collector's package for GTA 6, priced at $399.99. It is a premium physical bundle aimed at collectors, on top of the $79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate editions. Stock is limited, so check the Rockstar Store and major retailers for availability — and be wary of resale listings charging far above $399.99.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Paying $100 expecting the base game to cost that much. The base game is $79.99 — $100 is the Ultimate Edition price, and the $100 base-price rumor was wrong.
  • Buying the Ultimate Edition for extra story content. The extra $20 buys digital extras including the Vintage Vice City Pack, not a bigger story — Standard has the same campaign.
  • Expecting a sale soon. No sale has been announced, and Rockstar rarely discounts early — GTA V held near full price for years.
  • Buying it for online multiplayer on day one. There is no multiplayer at launch. If online is the reason you are buying, wait.
  • Paying resellers over $399.99 for the Vice City Collection. That is the official price — check the Rockstar Store and retailers for real stock first.
