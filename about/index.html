About ToolNova

Simple tools, done right — free forever, private by design.

What Is ToolNova?

ToolNova is a free online tools website created for the small, repetitive jobs everyone runs into: counting the words in an essay, generating a password you can actually trust, checking a BMI, pricing a loan, formatting JSON, or cleaning a list full of duplicates. Instead of hunting for a different site for each task — and closing five pop-ups on every one — you get them all in one fast, consistent place.

Our Principles

  • Free, for real. No sign-ups, no “pro” unlocks for basic features, no watermarks on downloads, no daily limits.
  • Private by architecture. Every ToolNova tool runs entirely in your web browser. We do not have a server receiving your text, images or files, which means we could not store or misuse them even if we wanted to.
  • Fast on any device. Pages are lightweight and mobile-first, so they load quickly on phones and modest connections, not just new desktops.
  • Accurate and honest. Calculators use the standard formulas and official unit definitions, and pages tell you plainly what a result does and does not mean (for example, that a BMI is a screening guide, not a diagnosis).

Who Builds ToolNova?

ToolNova is built and maintained by Adeel Abbas, a web developer and electronics technician who spends his days fixing real-world problems — from solar inverters to websites. The tools on this site started as utilities he kept rebuilding for clients and for himself; ToolNova is those utilities, polished and shared with everyone. The site is supported by advertising so the tools can stay free.

What’s Next

ToolNova launched with 15 tools and grows based on what visitors ask for. Upcoming additions are driven by requests from the contact page — if a tool would save you time every week, tell us about it.
