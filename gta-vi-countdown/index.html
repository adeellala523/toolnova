How the GTA 6 Countdown Timer Works

Before launch, this timer counted down to midnight on November 19, 2026 in your own time zone — because Rockstar never published one global unlock hour. The launch rolled around the world as each region hit midnight: New Zealand unlocked first (11:00 UTC on November 18), then Australia, Asia, Europe, and finally the Americas. Now that every region has passed midnight, the timer counts up instead: days, hours, minutes and seconds since GTA 6 came out. The "Since Trailer 1" number counts from December 4, 2023, the day Rockstar revealed the game — the full length of the wait.

How Much Does GTA 6 Cost?

The Standard Edition is $79.99 (£69.99, AU$129.95) and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 (£89.99, AU$159.95) — ten dollars above the usual $69.99 AAA price, but short of the $100 base price many rumors predicted. The Ultimate Edition adds digital extras including the Vintage Vice City Pack. There is also a physical collector's package, the Vice City Collection, at $399.99. Both editions were announced on June 24, 2026, and pre-orders opened the next day.

Is GTA 6 Coming to PC?

Not at launch — and Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a PC release date at all. The Rockstar Store sells only the PS5 and Xbox versions, there is no Steam, Epic or Rockstar Launcher listing, and Take-Two's own release slate through March 31, 2027 lists GTA VI for PS5 and Xbox only. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said only that "Rockstar Games has announced GTA 6 for console only so far."

The most specific PC claim anywhere comes from GamesBeat: on October 13, 2026 it reported, citing four anonymous industry sources, that a PC version is planned for the first quarter of 2027. Rockstar did not comment. Until Rockstar announces something, that is a report, not a date. The older pattern — GTA V took about 19 months to reach PC, Red Dead Redemption 2 about 13 — would point to late 2027 or 2028, but Rockstar has broken patterns before, so treat that as pattern-reading, not a prediction.

Warning: fake "GTA 6 PC download" sites are everywhere. Any site offering a GTA 6 APK, a PC download, or a browser version is fake — there is no official PC version to download, and those files are typically malware or scams. The only real copies are on PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store, and at retail.

GTA 6 Reviews at Launch

Critics rated it about as highly as a game can be rated: Metacritic 99 on PS5 (Universal Acclaim), OpenCritic 99 Top Critic Average with nearly every critic recommending it, and GameSpot 10/10. Reviews went live on November 18, the day before launch. The game also picked up eight nominations for The Game Awards, including Game of the Year, with the ceremony on December 10.

What to Do First in Vice City

  • Check your storage before you download. The pre-load was about 212 GB — clear space first, because the download plus day-one updates and saves need room beyond that.
  • Play the story first. Launch is single-player only, following Lucia and Jason — multiplayer is reported to arrive later, so the story is the whole game right now.
  • Lock down your accounts. A launch this big attracts phishing — turn on two-step verification on PlayStation Network, Xbox and Rockstar Games, and use a unique password for each. Our Password Generator makes one in a click.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Downloading a "GTA 6 PC" file. There is no PC version. Any APK, installer or browser build is fake — see the warning above.
  • Trusting exact unlock-hour claims. Rockstar never published an official unlock-hour table; launch was midnight local time per region. Sites quoting precise US unlock times or outage windows are passing on reports, not official facts.
  • Expecting a PS4 or Xbox One version. The game's scale targets current-generation hardware only — PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • Buying it for online play on day one. There is no multiplayer at launch. If online is the reason you are buying, wait for Rockstar's Online announcement.
  • Believing the old May 26, 2026 date. That was the first delay's date, announced May 2, 2025 — Rockstar moved it again on November 6, 2025, to November 19, 2026, the date the game actually came out on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days until GTA 6?

None — GTA 6 is out now. Grand Theft Auto VI launched on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, unlocking at midnight local time in each region. The countdown ended at launch; the live timer above now counts the time since release instead.

When did GTA 6 come out?

Thursday, November 19, 2026. Rockstar Games announced the date on November 6, 2025, after moving the game from its original 2025 window and then from May 26, 2026. The game unlocked at midnight local time in each region, with New Zealand first.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a PC release date. The game is only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A GamesBeat report from October 13, 2026, citing anonymous sources, says a PC version is planned for the first quarter of 2027 — Rockstar has not commented, so treat that as an unconfirmed report, not a date.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

The Standard Edition is $79.99 (£69.99) and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 (£89.99). There is also a physical collector's package, the Vice City Collection, priced at $399.99. Prices were announced with the editions on June 24, 2026.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

PlayStation 5 (including PS5 Pro) and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PlayStation 4, Xbox One or Nintendo Switch version, and no PC version at launch.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a criminal duo whose story plays out in Leonida — Rockstar's fictional US state — and its neon-soaked city of Vice City, a modern version of Miami. The story follows the pair after a simple score goes wrong.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

The pre-load was about 212 GB, so you need at least that much free space plus room for updates and save data. Digital pre-load opened on November 10, 2026 — PS5 at midnight local time and Xbox at 00:00 GMT — and code-in-box physical copies were sold from November 12.

Is GTA 6 multiplayer available at launch?

No — launch is the single-player story only. Rockstar has not announced a separate GTA VI Online date, and reports suggest multiplayer arrives in 2027. If you bought the game for online play, that part is still to come.
